# Tether UI audit

Audited the app at 390px, 768px, and 1280px. The screenshot sets are in `before/` and `after/` (24 captures each: eight states × three widths). Captures use 390×844 or 390×900, and 768×900 / 1280×900 viewports. “Before” files for suspect, stalled, offline, recovery, and recap often show `LIVE` or another phase; the capture did not wait for the requested state. That state mismatch is recorded as a defect rather than presented as a real stalled-state UI.

## Cross-state checks and fixes

- **Contrast:** the dim text token is 6.01:1 on Surface 1 and 5.57:1 on Surface 2. Live red is 5.61:1, stall amber 10.32:1, and feed mint 10.53:1 on Surface 1. Low-contrast metadata colors were replaced with the dim token; color still only encodes state.
- **Numbers:** tabular and lining numerals are applied across the UI, including elements with local font declarations.
- **Spacing and detail:** primary gutters and sheet/Two Lines padding use the 8px rhythm. Hairline rules, 4px chip insets, and 1px borders are optical details. Two Lines now has uppercase 11px / 0.08em headings, distinct trace states, one tick per feed event, and measured bandwidth counters.
- **Type loading:** the six active Fontsource WOFF2 faces are self-hosted and preloaded. Before preload, the phone column changed by 2px across font readiness; after preload, its measured height remained 644.375px before and after. Observed CLS was 0.00019.
- **Motion:** entry and event motion is capped at 220ms; the signal tick is 240ms. Reduced motion removes the slides and continuous tick, replacing entry motion with a short fade.
- **Icons and focus:** player glyphs use a consistent 16px, 1.5-stroke SVG treatment. Keyboard focus uses a visible 2px mint outline; a Tab check focused a button with the outline applied.
- **Touch targets:** player controls, sheet actions, language controls, sport controls, and disclosure summaries have 44px minimum targets.
- **States and copy:** idle, playing, suspect, stalled, offline, recovering, recap, and fullscreen are represented in the captures. The misleading player-state gate that hid the sheet during a real freeze was removed. Offline and no-event copy is direct and state-specific.
- **Visual effects:** no decorative glow or purple/blue gradient remains. The limited video gradients shade controls and loading artwork for legibility; the phone shadow was removed.

Portrait fullscreen keeps the 16:9 video uncropped, so black bars remain by design. The frozen frame and the recap remain inside the fullscreen wrapper.
